Unit 9: Chemical Transformations and Matter Balance 8 Hours
Prerequisites:
Chemical reaction, balancing equations, the mole, molar concentration.
Problem Situation:
During combustion, reactants disappear while new products form. How do we track the exact quantity of each species and determine which reactant runs out first?
ObjectivesContentsDidactic MaterialsActivities & Evaluation
  • Model a chemical transformation with a balanced equation.
  • Define reaction progress x (in mol).
  • Construct an ICE reaction progress table.
  • Identify limiting reactant and maximum progress x_max.
  • Establish matter balance in final state.
 I. Chemical Transformation (1. Initial and final state, 2. Reaction equation)
II. Progress of Reaction (1. Definition of progress x, 2. ICE progress table)
III. Matter Balance & Limiting Reactant (1. Hypotheses for x_max, 2. Limiting reactant determination, 3. Final composition)
  • Reaction of magnesium ribbon with hydrochloric acid
  • Gas collection apparatus over water with graduated test tube
  • Precipitation reaction: copper sulfate + sodium hydroxide
 Activities: React varied masses of Mg with fixed HCl volume; measure H₂ gas produced; verify that x_max matches theoretical limiting reactant calculation.
Evaluation: Full ICE table problem solving and end-of-year Supervised Exam 6.
